05 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad you
asked.

Have something else in mind?
Let’s talk about it ↗

I have an idea, but I’m not technical. Can you help?

Absolutely. Describe what you want your customers or team to do. We’ll help translate that into a practical scope, a clear user experience and the right technical approach.

Can you build more than a website?

Yes. We build portfolios, web applications, AI chatbots, calling assistants, agentic workflows, inventory systems, customer portals, databases and integrations. If it needs custom software, tell us about it and we’ll assess the best way forward.

Is everything custom built?

Your design and workflows are tailored to your needs. We use established frameworks and reliable services where they make sense, so you get a distinctive product without rebuilding every underlying tool.

How long does a project take?

A typical Starter website takes 10–14 days after scope and content are agreed. AI systems, integrations and larger applications need their own timeline. We agree on milestones before work starts.

What is included in the three months of support?

Post-launch bug fixes, content updates and performance support within the agreed project scope. Larger features and third-party subscription or usage fees are scoped separately, so there are no surprises.

Can you improve or connect my existing tools?

Yes. We can review your current website, database, CRM or operations workflow and propose improvements or integrations. Feasibility depends on the systems and API access available.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with founders and businesses worldwide. Choose USD or INR pricing above and tell us your time zone when planning your discovery call.
